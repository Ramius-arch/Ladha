TASTE OVER TREND

THE PHILOSOPHY

WE DON'T SELL USED CLOTHES.
WE EXCAVATE PERSONAL STYLE.

Nairobi is one of the fashion capitals of the African continent, but the retail landscape is choked by two extremes: cheap fast-fashion synthetic garbage that ends up in landfills after three washes, or inflated luxury boutiques charging 20x markups for logos.

Between 4:00 AM and sunrise, before Nairobi wakes up, thousands of shipping containers open across Gikomba, Toi, and Ngara. Inside are masterpieces of textile engineering: 1993 single-stitch cotton tees, 14-ounce American denim, Japanese nylon windbreakers, and tailored British wool.

LADHA was founded on a singular conviction: Your taste deserves better than the algorithm. We do the hours of dirt and digging so you get the pure unrepeatable heat.

THE 6% BENCHMARK

WHY 94% OF PIECES GET KILLED.

We inspect every prospective garment across four strict gates. If it fails even one criterion, it is immediately discarded:

1. FIBER INTEGRITY

Zero cheap poly-blends. We look for heavyweight ring-spun cotton, heavyweight wool, raw denim, and true vintage ripstop.

2. SILHOUETTE PROPORTION

It must drape with modern intent. Boxy shoulders, relaxed pit-to-pit, or fluid bias cuts that stand out on Nairobi streets.

3. HARDWARE & SEAMS

All zippers must be authentic YKK/Talon. Heavy bar-tack stitching, intact shank buttons, and zero broken threads.

4. UNREPEATABLE CHARACTER

No generic department store filler. Every piece on LADHA must stop someone mid-stride and trigger a conversation.

TRANSPARENCY STANDARDS

THE VINTAGE GRADING MATRIX

Because every piece is 1-of-1, we enforce rigorous standardized inspection grades so you know exactly what you are receiving before you order:

GRADE LEVEL SCORE STRUCTURAL CRITERIA PATINA DETAILS
GRADE A+ 9.6 – 10.0 Deadstock / unworn condition. Zero seam stress. Original crisp fabric hand feel. Pristine graphics and hardware.
GRADE A 9.0 – 9.5 Excellent structural integrity. Fully functional zips. Soft, pleasant natural vintage fading. Zero holes, tears, or stains.
DISTRESSED 8.5 – 8.9 Intentional wabi-sabi wear. Minor hem fraying. Authentic heavy stone-washing, paint speckling, or vintage honeycombs.
REJECTED < 8.5 Structural failures, pit stains, cheap poly. Never listed on LADHA. Recycled or re-purposed.

GARMENT CARE INSTRUCTION

Vintage fabrics carry decades of life. To keep your piece in pristine shape: Wash cold inside out (max 30°C), avoid synthetic heat tumble dryers (hang dry in shaded breeze), and steam rather than dry iron prints.
